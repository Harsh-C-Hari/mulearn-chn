About us

About µLearn CHN

µLearn is a learning network built on peer learning in small groups. Through it, learners find a community, mentors, interest groups, roadmaps, challenges and opportunities.

µLearn CHN is the student community at College of Engineering, Chengannur: our campus doorway into that network. We learn, build and grow together, and plug into what the wider µLearn community has to offer.

Across the µLearn network

What µLearn offers

Six things learners can tap into across the µLearn network. They belong to the wider µLearn ecosystem, and µLearn CHN is our campus doorway into them.

  • 01

    Learn with peers

    Community

    Find a community of learners to ask questions, share what you are building and learn alongside, in the small peer groups µLearn is built on.

    Open the µLearn community (opens in a new tab)

  • 02

    Guidance from people ahead of you

    Mentors

    Look up mentors on the µLearn platform when you want advice on what to learn next or feedback on what you have made.

    Find a mentor (opens in a new tab)

  • 03

    Go deeper in what you love

    Interest Groups

    Reach Level 4 to unlock Interest Group tasks, then choose a group. Groups sit under four families.

    • Coders
    • Creatives
    • Makers
    • Managers

    Explore Interest Groups (opens in a new tab)

  • 04

    Know what to learn next

    Roadmaps

    Follow guided learning roadmaps on the µLearn platform instead of working out your own path from scratch.

    Open Roadmaps (opens in a new tab)

  • 05

    Learn by doing, earn Karma

    Challenges

    Take on challenges and collect Karma points, the points µLearn uses to make learning feel like a game.

    Open Challenges (opens in a new tab)

  • 06

    See where learning can lead

    Opportunities

    Browse the career opportunities µLearn lists, and see how what you learn connects to real work.

    See opportunities (opens in a new tab)

Join µLearn CHN

Be a part of µLearn!

Getting started takes three steps. The first two are separate: a µLearn account gives you access to the wider network, and the CHN Discord is where our campus community meets. Do both.

  1. 01

    Create your µLearn account

    Sign up on the µLearn platform with email and password or Google sign-in. The account is your way into the wider µLearn network.

    Go to registration (opens in a new tab)

  2. 02

    Join the CHN Discord

    Join the Mulearn.CHN server to meet our campus community. This is a separate step: creating an account does not add you to the Discord.

    Open the CHN Discord invite (opens in a new tab)

  3. 03

    Explore Interest Groups

    Interest Group tasks unlock when you reach Level 4. Then you can choose a group under Coders, Creatives, Makers or Managers.

    Browse Interest Groups (opens in a new tab)
