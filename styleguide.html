01 Colour

Cream paper, navy ink, three loud inks.

Navy does every outline and every word. Indigo, yellow and mint carry the personality. Lavender and coral are flat tints borrowed from Board 2 and only ever hold navy text.

02 Typography

Big, tight and unapologetic.

Display type is set at 0.9 line height with negative tracking. Satoshi leads the display stack when it is installed; Plus Jakarta Sans 800 is the self-hosted fallback and carries all body text.

03 Spacing

An 8-pixel grid with room to breathe.

Every gap, padding and margin is a step on this scale. Sections use a fluid vertical rhythm of 64 to 140 pixels; page gutters flex from 20 to 80.

08 Decorative stickers

Flat shapes, dropped like stickers.

Asterisks, stars, triangles, squiggles and heavy ink shapes. Always flat, always aria-hidden, always clipped by their parent so pages never scroll sideways.

10 Focus-visible
